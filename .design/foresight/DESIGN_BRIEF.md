# Design Brief: Foresight (hackathon UI)

Status: active · Owner-approved direction (12 Oct 2026) · Supersedes the "Broadsheet" prototype in `design/prototype/` and the Stage 1 concepts in `design/concepts/`.

## Product

Foresight is an agent-native financial intelligence workspace (Agent Arena 2026, FinPilot track). Specialised agents ingest transactions, categorise, detect anomalies, forecast, simulate what-if decisions, propose interventions and check them before the user sees them. Positioning: **"Your money, a few steps ahead."** / "Clarity for what comes next."

## Named philosophy

**Calm premium fintech, "Atelier light".** Airy and optimistic, with editorial confidence: very pale blue-grey canvas, white rounded cards with hairline borders and barely-there shadows, serif display headlines and card titles, a clean sans for UI and numbers, one royal-blue primary, tinted circular icon chips, and a calm mountain landscape with the line "A calm mind builds a richer future." Inspired by Mercury (numerals, calm hierarchy), Linear (crisp rows, inspector panels), Ramp (reporting clarity) and Monarch (approachable cash-flow storytelling). Not a generic AI dashboard.

### Parameters

- **Canvas** very pale blue-grey; **surfaces** white; **ink** deep navy; secondary and faint text in muted slate.
- **Primary / forecast** royal blue (≈ #1F5EF0) with a soft blue likely-range band.
- **Scenario / hypothetical** dashed purple-magenta (CVD-safe against the blue).
- **Positive** green, **caution** amber, **risk** red: red is used only for a genuine shortfall.
- **Type**: Source Serif 4 (500/600) for display and card titles; Inter for UI; tabular numerals for money and dates.
- **Shape**: ~14px card radius, pill-shaped primary controls, circular icon chips.
- **Density**: generous around the hero chart; compact, aligned rows in tables and timelines.

## Content rules (non-negotiable; these intentionally differ from the mockups)

- **Truthful presentation:** visually distinguish observed history, computed metrics, probabilistic forecast (with "today" boundary and likely range), user hypotheticals, AI-written explanation and checked numbers. Every major number has an as-of date or timespan.
- **No regulated product advice:** no credit cards, savings accounts or other financial products. Advice concerns the user's own behaviour and timing.
- **No claims bank data can't support:** e.g. never "unused subscriptions". Use "overlap" or "rose in price".
- **No fake features:** no "Pro" upsell. The sign-in form is visible but disabled ("coming after the hackathon"). Primary actions are "Explore the live demo" and "Import a CSV statement".
- **"Checked", not "Verified"** in user-facing copy. Rejected drafts are shown honestly in the agent trail.
- Fictional person (Amara Nwosu), en-GB formatting, and a "Design prototype · fictional data" label on every page.

## Success criteria

A judge or user immediately reads it as a crafted premium product. The future (forecast and scenarios) dominates over history. Agent work and evidence are inspectable without clutter. Every figure reconciles across screens. The result is buildable in Next.js before 20 Oct 2026.
