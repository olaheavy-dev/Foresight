Foresight

A canvas the agents build for you, block by block, each one stamped with what kind of truth it holds.

Most finance apps give you fixed pages and a chat box on the side. In the Studio, every question, and every risk the agents spot on their own, produces a canvas: a forecast, a comparison, the transactions behind it, the options they tried and the check that approved one. The conversation stays in a narrow rail; the work takes the room.

Principles

The answer is a surface

A question returns a working canvas you can adjust and inspect, not a paragraph. Prose is one block among several.

Every block declares its truth

Observed, computed, forecast, hypothetical, AI-written or checked: a stamp on every block says which, before you read a number.

Agents work while you are away

Home is the canvas they assembled after the last change, with the steps they took listed beside it.

Show the rejected option

A plan you can trust shows the one that failed the check, and why. Rejection is part of the evidence, not an error.

Numbers from the engine only

Models write the words and the plans. Every figure is computed, recomputed by the Verifier, and traceable to rows.

Suggestions, never movements

Nothing moves money. Every action is “review and accept”, and dismissing is always one click.

Chart and data grammarLine style carries meaning, so colour is never the only cue

Observed

Solid ink

What happened, from your statement rows.

Forecast

Ink line, widening band

Likely range from 500 simulated paths, P10–P90.

Hypothetical

Dashed blue

Something you are testing. Never confused with what will happen.

Checked

Solid teal, always labelled

A plan the Verifier recomputed and approved.

Threshold

Dotted amber

Your buffer. Dropping under it is what raises a risk.

Boundary

Today rule

Left is observed, right is forecast. Always drawn, always labelled.
