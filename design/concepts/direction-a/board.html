Foresight Direction A · Intelligence Command Centre Design concept · fictional data

A morning briefing for your money: one calm outlook of the weeks ahead, the one thing that needs you, and a checked plan — with every number one click from its evidence.

Principles

The future is the heroThe outlook chart leads every visit. History is the short run-up on its left.
One thing at a timeA briefing ranks what matters today. Calm days say so; they never fill space with KPIs.
Every number has a kindObserved, computed, forecast, scenario, AI-written or checked — marked the same way everywhere.
Advice arrives checkedRecommendations show what was rejected and why. Nothing moves money; you decide.
Questions become reportsAsk something and get an analysis with a verdict, a chart, alternatives and evidence — not a chat bubble.
Quiet chromeNavigation recedes once you arrive; separators are felt, not seen.

Chart series are separated by line style and direct labels, not colour alone (the amber/teal pair is only 6.5 ΔE for protanopia, so both always carry an icon and a word).

Chart grammar — never violated

ObservedSolid ink. Only what your statements show.
ForecastSlate line + likely range band (10th–90th).
Your scenarioDashed blue. Hypothetical, never mixed with observed.
Previous forecastGrey dashed ghost after a revision.
Your bufferDotted amber, labelled at the axis.
Today + risk pointHard “today” rule; ringed, dated low.
